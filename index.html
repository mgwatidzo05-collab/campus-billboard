About the NUST Campus Event Billboard

The NUST Campus Event Billboard is a centralized platform designed to keep students informed about academic seminars, tech hackathons, club meetups, and social gatherings happening across the National University of Science and Technology. By creating an account, students gain instant access to verified campus happenings, helping you stay connected and engaged with student life.
