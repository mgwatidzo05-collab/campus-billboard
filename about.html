About the Platform

The NUST Campus Event Billboard is a centralized web platform designed to keep students informed about academic seminars, tech hackathons, club meetups, and social gatherings happening across the National University of Science and Technology. By requiring official student registration, the system ensures verified and secure engagement across campus life.
